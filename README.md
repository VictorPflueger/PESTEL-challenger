# PESTEL Challenger

AI agents complete a PESTEL analysis, challenge it, put a figure on each outside force and propose what to do or watch.

**Status: Phase 0, planning.** This repository holds the plan, [PLAN.md](PLAN.md), and nothing to run yet. The build starts with milestone M1 once the plan is approved. The tool will be served at <https://victorpflueger.github.io/PESTEL-challenger/>.

## What it will do

A person names a company and lists the outside forces they see under six headings: political, economic, social, technological, environmental and legal. AI agents complete and rate them, challenge the analysis and mark the dimensions under pressure. They put a figure on each threat with the KPIs of the company's sector, propose how to mitigate it, turn it into an advantage or watch it, and write a management summary with a watch list. The report downloads as a PDF, an HTML file and a JSON file for the Prototype Ideator.

## What is real and what is simulated

- **Simulation:** three fictional companies play without a key. Their own figures are invented and illustrative. What they say about the world, such as a law or a price, is real and shows its source and the date it was read.
- **Live run:** your company and your own Claude API key. You choose the model, the effort and a budget cap. The key and everything you enter stay in the tab's memory and are gone when you close or reload it; you keep a result only by downloading it.
- **Public information only:** never enter confidential, personal or otherwise protected data.
